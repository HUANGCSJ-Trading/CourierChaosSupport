PLAYER INFORMATION

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Courier Chaos is a general-audience iPhone delivery game developed by Huang Chun Hsi. This policy describes the game's local information, advertising, purchases, and support. It is specific to Courier Chaos, not Power Rush 10.

Local game information

The game stores coins, order completion, stars, equipment, music/sound/haptic settings, and language choice on your device. No game account is required. We do not operate a server for uploading or syncing these game saves, and the game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise-location access.

Local information remains until you remove the app or its data. Device backups and any later restoration are controlled by iOS and your Apple settings; we cannot manage or delete your Apple backups. Removing the app may permanently remove game progress.

Advertising and Google

The free version uses Google AdMob interstitial advertising at the end of successful orders. Rewards are saved before the ad; an unavailable ad does not prevent returning to order selection. Advertising personalization is disabled in the Google Mobile Ads configuration, but this does not mean advertising collects no information.

As documented by Google, its Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), app- or device-related identifiers, crash logs, performance information, advertising data such as impressions, and interactions with ads or the app. This information supports ad delivery and measurement, analytics, diagnostics, and protection against fraud or abuse. The game's local coin balance, cargo integrity, stars, and equipment save are not sent to Google by our game code. We do not add a separate analytics service or mediation network.

Third-party processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, Google's information about partner apps, and Google Mobile Ads data-disclosure documentation. Google manages retention of SDK information; we do not control that information or promise a fixed retention period. Google provides privacy controls and deletion-request routes described in its policy.

Your advertising choices

Google's User Messaging Platform checks applicable consent requirements before the game requests ads. Where required, it presents a consent form. You can revisit available advertising privacy choices in Workshop → Settings when the service requires a privacy-options entry point. Refusing consent or an unavailable consent service does not prevent gameplay, and ads are not requested when the SDK indicates that they are not permitted. The game does not request permission for cross-app tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Advertising personalization and Google's publisher first-party ID are disabled. The game requests Google's general-audience ad content rating. The optional permanent Remove Ads purchase suppresses level ads after the app verifies ownership. There are no additional coin rewards for clicking an ad or agreeing to personalized advertising.

Purchases and Apple

Apple processes the optional one-time, non-consumable Remove Ads purchase. The app verifies signed StoreKit transactions and checks the entitlement to apply or restore your purchase. We do not receive payment-card details. Apple handles transaction records, billing, and refunds under Apple's privacy policy. Restoring an entitlement does not sync local game progress.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send, use them to answer the request, and retain the correspondence as needed to resolve it and meet applicable obligations. Do not send sensitive information that is unnecessary for support. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any required legal retention.

This support website does not add advertising, analytics scripts, accounts, or forms. Its hosting provider processes the technical requests needed to deliver the pages; this is separate from the game's local save.

Children

Courier Chaos is not in the App Store Kids Category. It does not ask for a child's name, birth date, address, or contact information. Please contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information through support so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when the game's information practices change and update the effective date above. Developer: Huang Chun Hsi. For privacy or support questions, email huangcsj@icloud.com.

隱私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 10 日

Courier Chaos 是 Huang Chun Hsi 開發的一般受眾 iPhone 送貨遊戲。本政策適用於 Courier Chaos，不是 Power Rush 10 的政策。

裝置內的遊戲資料

金幣、過關紀錄、星數、裝備、音樂／音效／震動設定與語言選擇儲存在你的裝置。遊戲不需帳號，我們沒有提供上傳或同步遊戲進度的伺服器，也不要求相機、麥克風、聯絡人或精確定位權限。

資料保留至你移除 App 或資料為止。iOS 備份與還原由 Apple 及你的設定控制，我們無法管理或刪除 Apple 備份；刪除 App 可能永久失去遊戲進度。

Google 廣告

免費版會在成功派送領獎後顯示 AdMob 插頁廣告。金幣與進度先儲存，沒有廣告時不會卡住返回選關。Google Mobile Ads 的廣告個人化設定已停用，但這不表示廣告完全不處理資料。

依 Google 說明，其 SDK 可能處理 IP 位址與推估概略位置、App／裝置相關識別碼、當機紀錄、效能資訊、廣告曝光及互動資料，以提供與衡量廣告、分析、診斷及防止詐欺或濫用。我們的遊戲程式不會把本機金幣、貨物完整度、星數或裝備存檔傳給 Google，也未加入獨立分析服務或其他廣告中介網路。

第三方處理適用 Google 隱私政策、Google 合作 App 資料說明及 Google Mobile Ads 資料揭露。SDK 資料的保留與刪除由 Google 管理，我們不能承諾固定保留期限；請使用 Google 政策提供的隱私控制或刪除申請方式。

廣告隱私選擇

遊戲請求廣告前會透過 Google User Messaging Platform 檢查適用的同意要求，必要時顯示同意表單。服務要求提供隱私選項入口時，可在「裝備工坊 → 設定」重新開啟。拒絕同意或服務無法使用不影響遊玩；SDK 指示不允許請求廣告時不會請求。遊戲不會顯示 Apple 跨 App 追蹤授權提示。

選購永久移除廣告並驗證購買後，會跳過關卡廣告。點擊廣告或同意個人化廣告不會得到額外金幣。

Apple 購買

永久無廣告為選購的一次性非消耗型商品。Apple 處理付款，App 驗證 StoreKit 簽署交易及購買權益，以啟用或恢復購買。我們不會取得信用卡資料。交易紀錄、退款與帳務由 Apple 依 Apple 隱私政策處理。恢復購買不會同步本機遊戲進度。

客服與網站

寄信給客服時，我們會收到你選擇提供的信箱及內容，僅用於回應問題，並依處理需要及適用義務保留。請勿寄送不必要的敏感資料；你可來信要求刪除客服往來，法定保留要求除外。本網站沒有另加廣告、分析腳本、帳號或表單；主機服務商會處理提供網頁所需的技術請求，與遊戲本機存檔無關。

兒童、更新與聯絡

本遊戲不屬於 App Store 兒童類別，不要求兒童姓名、生日、住址或聯絡資料。若你認為兒童已透過客服提供個資，請聯絡我們。資料處理方式變更時會更新本政策與生效日期。
