Quick answers

How do I start playing?

Choose Start Delivery, tap the first unlocked town, and select an available order. Hold the throttle to ride; use steering and the brake to protect your cargo. New players begin with the first order and unlock the route forward by making successful deliveries.

When are my coins saved?

After a successful delivery, tap Claim Coins. The reward and progress are saved before a single end-of-level ad is shown. Closing the ad returns you to the same town's order selection. If an ad is unavailable or cannot be presented, you keep your coins and return without waiting for one.

How does the perfect-delivery reward work?

A successful delivery with exactly 100% cargo integrity earns a perfect-delivery celebration and twice the normal coin reward. Coins are game currency only, not real money.

How do I remove or restore ads?

Choose Remove Ads on the home screen for the optional permanent, one-time purchase. It is not a subscription and does not change rewards or difficulty. The U.S. base price is US$4.99; Apple displays your local price before purchase. Restore Purchases is available in the purchase panel and in Workshop → Settings. Use the Apple Account associated with the purchase and an internet connection.

Can I move my progress to another device?

Game progress, coins, equipment, settings, and language choice are stored locally on the current iPhone. The game does not provide an account or its own cloud-sync service. Restoring the ad-free purchase does not restore game progress. Please avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting.

Where are language and privacy settings?

Tap the globe on the home screen to select one of ten languages. Open Workshop → Settings for music, sound, haptics, and privacy links. Advertising privacy choices appear there when Google's consent service requires them for your region.

金幣與廣告

成功派送後按「領取金幣」，獎勵與進度會先儲存，再顯示一次關卡廣告。關閉廣告後回到同一小鎮選關；沒有廣告可播時直接返回，金幣不會消失。貨物完整度恰好 100% 的成功派送可獲雙倍遊戲金幣，不能兌換現金。

永久無廣告

首頁「移除廣告」提供一次買斷，非訂閱。美國基準價 US$4.99，實際價格以 Apple 購買畫面為準。購買視窗與「裝備工坊 → 設定」均可恢復購買；使用原購買的 Apple 帳號並連上網路。移除廣告不影響獎勵或難度。

進度與語言

遊戲進度保存在目前 iPhone，沒有遊戲帳號或自行提供的雲端同步。恢復購買不等於恢復遊戲進度；排查問題時請先不要刪除 App。首頁地球圖示可切換十種語言。
